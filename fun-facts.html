Fun Facts

Interesting things about the zodiac.

1

The word zodiac comes from a Greek phrase meaning circle of animals.

2

The zodiac has 12 signs, and each one covers about 30 degrees of the sky.

3

The zodiac signs we use today trace back to ancient Babylon, more than 2,000 years ago.

4

The zodiac constellations lie along the ecliptic, the path the Sun appears to take across the sky.

5

The Sun also passes through a constellation called Ophiuchus, but it is not one of the 12 astrological signs.

6

Libra is the only sign symbolized by an object, the scales. The others are animals or people.

7

The signs are grouped into four elements: fire, earth, air and water.

8

Because the sky slowly shifts over time, the constellations no longer line up exactly with the sign dates.

9

The Chinese zodiac is a different system. It uses 12 animals in a 12-year cycle instead of 12 monthly signs.

10

Astrology is not supported by science. Enjoy it as entertainment, not as advice.
