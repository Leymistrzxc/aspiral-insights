The Four Elements

Each zodiac sign belongs to one of four elements, and each element has its own character.

Fire

Energetic, passionate and bold. Fire signs start things and inspire others.

  • Signs: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius
  • Keywords: Energy, courage, enthusiasm
  • Goes well with: Air feeds fire, so Fire and Air signs get along well.

Earth

Practical, patient and dependable. Earth signs build steady, lasting things.

  • Signs: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn
  • Keywords: Stability, loyalty, common sense
  • Goes well with: Earth and Water support each other, so they often match well.

Air

Curious, sociable and full of ideas. Air signs love to talk and connect.

  • Signs: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius
  • Keywords: Ideas, communication, curiosity
  • Goes well with: Air and Fire fuel each other, and Air signs also enjoy other Air signs.

Water

Intuitive, emotional and caring. Water signs feel deeply and read people well.

  • Signs: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces
  • Keywords: Feelings, intuition, empathy
  • Goes well with: Water and Earth nurture each other, and Water signs understand other Water signs.
