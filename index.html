What Is the Zodiac?

The zodiac is a circle of twelve signs, each covering about 30 degrees of the sky. Each sign is linked to a season of the year, an element and a set of personality traits. Find yours below, or use the Birthday Finder.

The Twelve Signs

Aries

Mar 21 – Apr 19

Aries opens the zodiac year, and it acts like it.

Taurus

Apr 20 – May 20

Taurus is steady, sensual and deeply loyal.

Gemini

May 21 – Jun 20

Gemini is curious, quick and endlessly talkative.

Cancer

Jun 21 – Jul 22

Cancer is intuitive, nurturing and fiercely protective of the people they love.

Leo

Jul 23 – Aug 22

Leo is warm, generous and born to be noticed.

Virgo

Aug 23 – Sep 22

Virgo is thoughtful, precise and quietly devoted.

Libra

Sep 23 – Oct 22

Libra seeks balance, beauty and fairness.

Scorpio

Oct 23 – Nov 21

Scorpio is intense, perceptive and fiercely private.

Sagittarius

Nov 22 – Dec 21

Sagittarius is a restless optimist with a taste for travel and big questions.

Capricorn

Dec 22 – Jan 19

Capricorn is disciplined, patient and quietly ambitious.

Aquarius

Jan 20 – Feb 18

Aquarius is original, independent and a little ahead of its time.

Pisces

Feb 19 – Mar 20

Pisces is gentle, imaginative and deeply empathetic.

Featured Fun Facts

Circle of animals

The word zodiac comes from a Greek phrase meaning circle of animals.

Four elements

The twelve signs are grouped into fire, earth, air and water.

Only one object

Libra is the only sign symbolized by an object, the scales.
