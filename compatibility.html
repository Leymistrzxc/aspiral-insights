How the Percentage Is Worked Out

It depends on the distance between two signs on the zodiac wheel.

  • Same sign: 75%
  • Next to each other: 50%
  • Two signs apart: 85%
  • Three signs apart: 60%
  • Four signs apart: 90%
  • Five signs apart: 45%
  • Opposite signs: 70%
